The two sides of a testimonial

What your customer does

  1. Receives an email or scans a QR code.
  2. Opens a single-use link on their phone.
  3. Records a short video.
  4. Confirms consent and submits the clip for review.

What your team does

  1. Creates the invite for a specific customer.
  2. Reviews the recording that comes back.
  3. Trims it to the part worth keeping.
  4. Approves it, or holds it back.
  5. Places the gallery embed on the website.

03

Consent is part of submitting

Before the video is submitted, the customer confirms they agree to it being reviewed and, if your team approves it, shown on your website. ListenUp is the tool for that step. Your business still chooses the wording you rely on and follows the privacy rules that apply where you operate.

Your staff decide what is fit to show

A recording arrives for review. Someone on your team can trim it, then approve it or hold it back. Approved videos are the ones the gallery is allowed to show. A video you have not approved stays off the website.

Questions worth asking us

Do customers install an app?

They open the invite link on their phone and record from there. The recording step is part of that link. They do not create a ListenUp account.

Can a video appear on our website by itself?

Someone on your team approves it first. The gallery shows approved videos. A clip you have not approved stays out of the gallery.

What does the consent step cover?

It asks the customer to confirm consent before submitting. It records that agreement as part of the workflow. It does not replace a privacy review for your business, your wording, or the rules in the places you operate.

How does the gallery get onto our site?

Copy an iframe or a script embed onto a page you control. On WordPress, use a Custom HTML block. We can walk through the placement with you.

Is the pricing on this site final?

No. Starter, Growth, and Business are working names, and the amounts are examples. Read the pricing note, then contact us before you plan a budget around those figures.
