A practical path from ask to website

ListenUp is built for the ordinary case: a business already talking to customers, and a website that should show a few honest videos.

  • One customer, one link

    Send an email or share a QR code. Each invite is single-use, so the recording step belongs to that customer.

  • Consent at submission

    The customer confirms they agree before the video comes to your team for review.

  • Approval before it is public

    Staff trim and approve first. The gallery can show the videos your team has cleared.

How it works

Four steps. Your customer records. Your team decides what is fit to publish.

  1. Invite

    Email a customer, or let them scan a QR code. Both open a single-use link.

    Invites in detail

  2. Record

    They record a short testimonial on their phone and confirm consent.

    Recording and consent

  3. Review

    Your staff watch the clip, trim it, and approve or hold it.

    Review and publish

  4. Embed

    Approved videos appear in a gallery you add to your site with an iframe or a script.

    Gallery embed

A fit for teams who publish on their own site

ListenUp is for a business that wants real customer video on its own website, with a person approving each clip.

  • You can ask in person or by email

    A QR code covers the counter, the job site, or the end of a visit. Email covers everyone else.

  • A person approves the cut

    Someone on staff chooses the part worth keeping and whether it belongs on the site.

  • Your pages stay yours

    The gallery sits inside a page you control, including a WordPress page that can hold an embed.

The gallery lives on your website

Visitors watch approved videos on your page. You add the gallery with an iframe or a script. On WordPress, a Custom HTML block is enough.
